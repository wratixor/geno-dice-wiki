# Curse

Curse takes hold on the body and steadily erodes Endurance; the Curse anti-heal rider weakens Endurance healing only and does not reduce discrete Guard gain.

Primary: Technique; Support: Onslaught; Auxiliary: Spirit.

## Normal · I–III

Curse pressures Endurance with an initial hit and lingering damage; the Curse anti-heal rider strongly cuts Endurance healing for one round.

```text
Initial Physical = 89.76×Q(Onslaught); DoT total = 97.92×Q(Technique); anti-heal reduction = neutral + 0.15×bias(Spirit/Body)
```

## Ultimate · IV–VI

Ultimate strengthens the anti-heal role. Overload deepens and extends the Curse anti-heal rider but does not multiply the DoT again; Curse anti-heal does not scale discrete Guard gain.

```text
Initial Physical = 168.87×Q(Onslaught); DoT total = 207.84×Q(Technique); Ultimate/Overload anti-heal uses its neutral anchor + 0.15×bias(Spirit/Body)
```
