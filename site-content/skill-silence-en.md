# Silence

Silence temporarily disrupts speech: the voice returns after two full rounds at I–III or three at IV–VI. Resolve cannot restore a missing voice.

Primary: Technique; Support: Spirit; Auxiliary: Onslaught.

## Normal · I–III

Blocks speech, not magic, for two full rounds and deals a small Psychic hit. The application round end is skipped. Archived battles retain legacy charges.

```text
Psychic = 29.376 × [0.70 Q(Technique) + 0.30 Q(Spirit) × E25(Onslaught/Self-Control)]; Silence duration unchanged
```

## Ultimate · IV–VI

At tiers IV–VI speech is blocked for three full rounds. Overload keeps this duration instead of accumulating charges.

```text
Psychic = 62.352 × [0.70 Q(Technique) + 0.30 Q(Spirit) × E25(Onslaught/Self-Control)]; STAGGER/Overload control riders unchanged
```

Live duels use 2 full rounds of Silence at I–III and 3 at IV–VI. Archived simulations may use a different rules version.
