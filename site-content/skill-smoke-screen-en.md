# Smoke Screen

Smoke does not conjure armour: it makes direct Physical attacks glance off and buys time to take position after the current round.

Primary: Reaction; Support: Self Control; Auxiliary: Body.

## Normal · I–III

Smoke softens direct Physical hits this round, including Guard-bypassing hits, without consuming existing Guard; after it clears, gain +1 Guard.

```text
quality = 0.70 Q80(Reaction) + 0.30 Q80(Self-Control) × E25(Body/Spirit); direct Physical multiplier = 1 - SmokeCurve(quality); end round +1 Guard
```

## Ultimate · IV–VI

Ultimate turns the time won by smoke into preparation for the next action: after the round it grants +1 Guard and +1 Impulse. Overload spends 1 Impulse for one additional Guard and keeps the ordinary Ultimate +1 Impulse payoff.

```text
same stat-scaled Smoke; Ultimate end-round Guard/Impulse riders and Overload extra Guard unchanged
```
