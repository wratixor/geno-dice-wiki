# Regeneration

A natural process restores the body over time; excess healing does not create combat Guard by itself.

Primary: Self Control; Support: Body; Auxiliary: Technique.

## Normal · I–III

Slow natural bodily recovery.

```text
Endurance HoT total = 130.56 × [0.70 Q(Self-Control) + 0.30 Q(Body)]; Technique/Reaction bias redistributes the fixed total across 3 ticks
```

## Ultimate · IV–VI

Ultimate restores more Endurance, but excess bodily healing no longer becomes Guard.

```text
Endurance HoT total = 389.7 × [0.70 Q(Self-Control) + 0.30 Q(Body)]; same redistribution; Overload frontloads one scheduled tick
```
