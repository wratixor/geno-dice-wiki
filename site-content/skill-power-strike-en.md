# Power Strike

Body supplies the hit and Onslaught the crush; empowerment spends prepared Guard, not health.

Primary: Body; Support: Onslaught; Auxiliary: Reaction.

## Normal · I–III

Strong non-MAGIC hit; execution grants +1 Impulse.

```text
Physical = 125.664 × [0.70 Q(Body) + 0.30 Q(Onslaught) × E25(Reaction/Technique)]
```

## Ultimate · IV–VI

Empowered Face converts prepared Guard into a crush.

```text
Physical = 181.86 × [0.70 Q(Body) + 0.30 Q(Onslaught) × E25(Reaction/Technique)]; Overload keeps the Guard-crush rider
```
