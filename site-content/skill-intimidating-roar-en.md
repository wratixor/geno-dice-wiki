# Intimidating Roar

The roar pressures Psyche without magic and establishes Fear only when will is already exposed or Psyche is shaken; it does not drain Impulse itself.

Primary: Onslaught; Support: Body; Auxiliary: Reaction.

## Normal · I–III

Control and pressure on an already shaken target, not primary Psychic DPS.

```text
Psychic = 73.44 × [0.70 Q(Onslaught) + 0.30 Q(Body) × E25(Reaction/Technique)]; FEAR riders unchanged
```

## Ultimate · IV–VI

Ultimate strengthens control; the Roar itself does not drain Impulse.

```text
Psychic = 142.89 × [0.70 Q(Onslaught) + 0.30 Q(Body) × E25(Reaction/Technique)]; Ultimate/Overload FEAR riders unchanged
```
