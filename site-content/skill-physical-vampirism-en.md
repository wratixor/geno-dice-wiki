# Physical Vampirism

Physical Vampirism requires real contact. Overload spends the user's Guard on a riskier contact and amplifies damage only against a target that was already open; it does not break Guard by itself.

Primary: Spirit; Support: Technique; Auxiliary: Self Control.

## Normal · I–III

Drain uses actual Endurance damage dealt.

```text
Physical raw damage = 148.512×Q(Spirit); heal fraction = VampCurve(Technique)×E30(Self-Control/Onslaught)
```

## Ultimate · IV–VI

Ultimate finishes an open target. Overload spends 1 Guard and adds another damage multiplier only when the target was already open before the action; the overload itself does not crush target Guard.

```text
Physical raw damage = 207.84×Q(Spirit), ×1.35 on open target; Overload open target applies the second ×1.35; heal uses actual damage × VampCurve(Technique) × E30(Self-Control/Onslaught)
```
