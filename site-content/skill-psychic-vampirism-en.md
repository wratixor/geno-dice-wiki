# Psychic Vampirism

Magic transfers Psyche actually taken; stealing prepared Impulse requires an explicit Empower.

Primary: Spirit; Support: Technique; Auxiliary: Self Control.

## Normal · I–III

Psychic drain acts directly through Psyche.

```text
Psychic raw damage = 130.56×Q(Spirit); heal fraction = VampCurve(Technique)×E30(Self-Control/Onslaught)
```

## Ultimate · IV–VI

Overheal no longer mints Impulse; stealing enemy Impulse is separately purchased through Empower.

```text
Psychic raw damage = 285.78×Q(Spirit); heal uses actual damage × VampCurve(Technique) × E30(Self-Control/Onslaught); Overload Impulse-steal rider unchanged
```
