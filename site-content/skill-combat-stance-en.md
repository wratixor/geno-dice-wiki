# Combat Stance

Combat Stance is bodily preparation: one Guard receives a Physical hit and proper positioning strengthens the next Physical response.

Primary: Body; Support: Self Control; Auxiliary: Reaction.

## Normal · I–III

Combat Stance grants one Guard charge and prepares the next Physical response.

```text
Guard +1; next Physical bash = 122.4 × [0.70 Q(Body) + 0.30 Q(Self-Control) × E25(Reaction/Technique)]
```

## Ultimate · IV–VI

While Fortified is active, ordinary Physical damage cannot remove the last Guard; an overloaded Physical action still can. Overload is pre-declaration preparation: its Impulse is paid on declaration, the ordinary +1 Guard is received before initiative resolves, and that Guard is not granted again on execution.

```text
Guard +1; FORTIFIED; bash = 259.8 × [0.70 Q(Body) + 0.30 Q(Self-Control) × E25(Reaction/Technique)]; Overload pre-declaration Guard unchanged
```
