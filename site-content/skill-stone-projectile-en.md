# Stone Barrage

The stones are created by magic but remain separate physical objects: each meets Guard on its own and the third stone requires Impulse overload.

Primary: Spirit; Support: Onslaught; Auxiliary: Reaction.

## Normal · I–III

Each stone meets Guard separately. Empower buys a third stone instead of hidden penetration.

```text
Cost 1 Impulse; PrimaryBudget = 2×74.256×Q(Spirit); hit1=PrimaryBudget/2; hit2=hit1×E30(Reaction/Technique)
```

## Ultimate · IV–VI

Ultimate rewards a real same-action barrage breach through Guard while remaining two base hits.

```text
Cost 1 Impulse; PrimaryBudget = 2×135.096×Q(Spirit); hit1=PrimaryBudget/2; hit2=hit1×E30; Overload hit3=135.096×Q(Onslaught)×E30; STAGGER breach rider unchanged
```
