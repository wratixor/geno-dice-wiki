# Feinting Thrust

Feinting Thrust exploits the predictability of prepared Guard; against an open target the trick adds little.

Primary: Technique; Support: Body; Auxiliary: Onslaught.

## Normal · I–III

The feint is intentionally modest into an open target. Against Guard, the false reaction spends 1 Guard and the real thrust bypasses the remainder.

```text
Open = 79.56 × Q(Body) × E30(Onslaught/Self-Control); guarded adds 22.44 × Q(Technique)
```

## Ultimate · IV–VI

Ultimate is anti-tank: open-target damage is lower than damage after Guard bait; current coefficients are shown in the formula. Overload spends 1 Guard at declaration and attempts to strip one additional Guard; it grants no defensive evasion.

```text
Open = 125.386 × Q(Body) × E30(Onslaught/Self-Control); guarded adds 69.464 × Q(Technique); Overload strips 1 Guard before damage
```
